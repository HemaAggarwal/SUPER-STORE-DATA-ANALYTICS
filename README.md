## 📊 Key Insights
- Analyzed overall business performance using **$445K in total sales, 6,402 units sold, $59.5K profit, and an average delivery time of 103 days**, providing a comprehensive overview of store performance.
- Compared monthly **sales and profit trends** across **2019 and 2020**, helping identify seasonal patterns, business growth, and periods of declining performance.
- Evaluated product performance across different **categories and sub-categories**, where **Office Supplies** generated the highest sales among categories, while **Phones** emerged as the top-performing sub-category.
- Analyzed customer purchasing behavior based on **shipping modes**, revealing that **Standard Class** was the most preferred shipping option, contributing the highest sales.
- Compared sales performance across different **regions**, with the **West region contributing the highest share (35%)**, followed by **East (26%)**, **Central (21%)**, and **South (17%)**, helping identify high-performing markets.
- Examined customer distribution by **segment**, where the **Consumer segment accounted for the largest share (48%)**, followed by **Corporate (30%)** and **Home Office (22%)**, providing insights into customer demographics.
- Analyzed payment preferences and found that **Cash on Delivery (COD)** was the most commonly used payment method (**41%**), followed by **Online Payments (36%)** and **Cards (23%)**.
- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with dynamic region filters, KPI cards, maps, and visualizations, enabling users to monitor sales performance, profit trends, customer segments, and regional performance for data-driven business decision-making.
- Tools Used: Power BI | Excel | DAX | Power Query.
